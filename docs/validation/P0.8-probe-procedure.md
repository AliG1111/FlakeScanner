# P0.8 Probe procedure (owner, at the forming line)

Written for the probe build of P0.7 (Settings › Diagnostics). Plain steps; nothing here needs a computer at the line.
Everything the probe saves stays on the phone until you share the zip. Plant pictures never go into the repository.

## Before going to the line (office, 10 minutes)

1. Install the build (README, step 3) and open **Settings / About › Diagnostics**. Allow the camera.
2. Tap **Camera report**. It lists every camera and format. Nothing else is needed yet.
3. Tap **LiDAR probe** on a table 0.5 to 1 m under the phone with a tape measure laid beside it; type the tape-measure distance
   (camera lens to table, in mm) in the box first. This checks the switch to the LiDAR camera and the distance.

## At the line (30 to 40 minutes)

Hold the phone, or mount it as it will be, looking straight down at the mat, at the height it will be used (measure that
height with the tape and write it down). **Setup**: shutter limit 1/2000 s (try 1/4000 s if the picture is bright), region width 50 %.

| Step | Button | What to do | Needed from |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sample frames** (8 frames) | Mat moving at its normal speed. Run it 3 times: the usual speed, then the fastest and the slowest speeds you will use. Write the line speed beside each run. | flake pictures (Q-09), blur, banding |
| 2 | **Light check** | Mat moving. Run twice. | flicker of the plant light |
| 3 | **Readout test** | Point at a **still**, evenly lit surface (a clean board or the stopped mat). Follow the prompt: turn the phone half a turn about the lens axis, tap **Continue**. | readout time and direction |
| 4 | **Noise series** | Still surface, light as at the line. About 30 s. Keep the phone steady. | noise per ISO |
| 5 | **LiDAR probe** | Over the mat at the real mounting height; type the tape-measure height. Run again at a second height (about 30 cm different), and once with the phone pointing at a flat spot near the mat's edge. | distance accuracy, zone readings |
| 6 | **Camera report** | Once. | format list |

Also note: the plant light level (a lux meter app is fine, reading on the mat), and the lamp type if known.

## After

1. Open Diagnostics, tap **Make the zip to share**, then **Share the zip** and send it to your PC (AirDrop is not available on Windows:
   use **Save to Files › iCloud Drive**, e-mail, or OneDrive). Do not put it in the repository.
2. Give the zip to the project thread with your notes (heights, speeds, light level). An agent analyses it and records the
   result in `docs/validation/`; the design is updated before P1.
3. In Diagnostics, tap **Delete all results** when the zip is safe.
